# Sign-in and guest access

## Description

Sign-in connects users to their organization and loads the access allowed by their role. The app supports the organization's regular account sign-in flow and time-limited guest access through invitation links or QR codes. Guest access lets a group join without creating a permanent account for every participant.

## Sign in with a regular account

1. Open The House App or your organization's sign-in link.
2. Choose your organization when prompted.
3. Continue through the account sign-in screen.
4. After sign-in, the app loads the pages and actions allowed by your role.

## Join as a guest

1. Scan the QR code or open the invitation link supplied by an administrator.
2. Follow the guest sign-in prompt.
3. Use the app within the access and expiration limits set for that guest group.

Guest access is temporary. Logging out can end access to the guest account, and signing in again requires a valid invitation.
