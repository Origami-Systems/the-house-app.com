# Houses and house profiles

## Description

The Houses page lists every house in the organization and shows each house's identity and current point total. Opening a house shows its color, description, motto, leaders, deans, and recent activity. This gives students and staff one place to learn about a house and follow its progress.

## How to view a house

1. Select **Houses** in the main navigation.
2. Select a house tile to open that house's profile.
3. Review its motto, recent points, deans, leaders, and other house information.
4. Select the points feed to see the house's full point history.

## Buttons and controls

- A **house tile** opens that house's profile.
- **Add Points** opens the point-awarding form for users who have permission to award points to the selected house.
- Entries in the **recent points feed** open the details for that point award or deduction.
