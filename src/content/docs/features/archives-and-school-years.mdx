# Archives and school years

## Description

Archives preserve records that are no longer part of the active school year. Administrators can review archived users, houses, games, and game statistics. Starting a new year archives the current year and resets active points and announcements so the next competition begins with a clean slate.

## View archived records

1. Open **Admin > Archives**.
2. Select **View Archived Users**, **View Archived Houses**, or **View Archived Games and Stats**.
3. Select a game to see its type, cheering setting, dates, winner, and available cheer statistics.

Archive categories with no records are hidden. If every category is empty, the page displays **Nothing in the archive**.

## Start a new year

1. Open **Admin** and find the **Management** card.
2. Select **Start New Year**.
3. Read and complete the confirmation.

Starting a new year archives the existing year and resets current points and announcements. Treat it as an end-of-year action, not a way to correct an individual point or announcement.
