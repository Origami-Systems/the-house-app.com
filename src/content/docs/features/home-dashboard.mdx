# Home dashboard

## Description

The home dashboard gives house members a quick view of their own house. It shows the house motto, recent points, house details, deans, and student leaders. Users who do not belong to a house are sent to the full Houses page instead.

## How to use it

1. Select **Home** in the main navigation.
2. Read the recent points feed to see the latest activity for your house.
3. Select the points feed to open the full point history.
4. Use the house information below the feed to see your house's deans and student leaders.

## Buttons and controls

- **Add Points** opens the point-awarding form. It only appears when your role is allowed to award points to this house.
- Selecting a point opens its full details and comments.

If your account is not assigned to a house, the app opens the Houses page instead of the dashboard.
