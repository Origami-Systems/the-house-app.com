# Notifications

## Description

Notifications keep users informed about new announcements and point activity. Users can turn device notifications on or off and choose which supported notification types they want to receive. Notification controls are available when the app is installed on a compatible device.

## Turn notifications on

1. Install The House App on your device.
2. Open **Account Settings > Manage Notifications**.
3. Use the notification button to enable notifications.
4. Accept the permission request from your browser or operating system.
5. Turn **Announcements** and **Points** on or off as needed.
6. Select **Save Changes**.

The Points option only appears when your account belongs to a house.

## Buttons and controls

- The notification button subscribes or unsubscribes this device.
- **Announcements** controls alerts for new announcements.
- **Points** controls alerts when your house earns points.
- **Save Changes** stores changes to the notification types. It appears after you change a setting.

If you denied notification permission at the device or browser level, change that permission in the device settings before trying again.
